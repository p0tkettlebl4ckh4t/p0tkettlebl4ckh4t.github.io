Rainy Sunday

May 16, 2025 2-minute read
PotKettleBlackHat
Life

It has rained pretty much all day.

I had planned to go out this morning but when I opened the curtains it looked like the entire street had been abandoned, so I went back to bed instead. Woke up again around eleven and spent another twenty minutes convincing myself that getting up was worth it.

Eventually I did.

Made some toast, cleaned the kitchen and then went into town to get a few things. I only needed shampoo and toothpaste but somehow came home with a new hoodie and a takeaway. This happens every time I go shopping.

I’ve also been spending a bit more time on my computer lately. Mostly nothing exciting. I’ve been trying to organise some old files because apparently my system for storing things is just putting everything on the desktop and hoping future me knows what past me was thinking.

Future me does not.

Had a little argument with someone online this afternoon too. He was trying to tell me I couldn’t possibly know anything about him because he had “privacy settings”.

I laughed at that.

I didn’t bother explaining that if I really wanted to find someone, I’d probably have a pretty good idea where to start. People leave more information online than they realise. I’ve found people before just from stupid little details they thought meant nothing.

Didn’t actually do anything to him, obviously. I couldn’t be bothered.

He blocked me anyway.

Honestly, some people get very dramatic about the internet.

The rest of tonight is probably going to be a film and some food. I bought ingredients to cook something properly but there’s a strong chance they’ll remain in the fridge while I order a pizza.

We’ll see.
