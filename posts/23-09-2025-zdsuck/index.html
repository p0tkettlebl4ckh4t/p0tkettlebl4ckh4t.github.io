ZeroDays Again

September 23, 2025 2-minute read
PotKettleBlackHat
Internet

I’ve seen people talking about ZeroDays again.

I genuinely don’t understand why people take those guys so seriously.

They’re skids.

That’s it.

I’ve watched people in that group spend hours talking themselves up over things that wouldn’t impress anyone who actually knew what they were doing. They seem to think using dramatic usernames and posting screenshots makes them some kind of elite group.

It doesn’t.

I normally wouldn’t bother mentioning them, but someone sent me a message asking if I was worried because apparently ZeroDays had been “looking into me”.

I laughed.

If they’re looking into me, they’re doing a terrible job.

I know exactly who a couple of them are anyway.

Not going to say how because I’m not giving them free lessons.

The funny part is that the person who messaged me clearly expected me to panic. Instead I was eating a sandwich and watching a documentary.

This is the thing with the internet. Everyone wants to sound dangerous.

I don’t really care about being dangerous. I just don’t like people pretending they’re something they’re not.

I’ve been doing random computer stuff for years, and I’m perfectly happy admitting I don’t know everything. That’s apparently a controversial opinion online.

Anyway, outside of that, my week has been pretty normal.

Went shopping yesterday and bought far too many snacks. I also tried a new coffee place near me, which was actually pretty good. Their iced coffee is probably the best I’ve had recently.

I’m considering going back tomorrow.

Maybe I’ll write something more interesting next time.

Or maybe ZeroDays will finally manage to “hack” me.

I’ll keep everyone updated.
