December Lights

December 8, 2025 2-minute read
PotKettleBlackHat
Life • Winter

Put the Christmas lights up today.

I say “put them up” like it was a simple job. It took nearly two hours because they were tangled into a shape that shouldn’t be possible in normal space.

Every year I put them away properly. Every year they come back like this.

I’m not ruling out interference.

Once they were finally working, one section started blinking at a different speed from the rest. My first thought was that somebody was trying to send a message through them.

I watched it for about ten minutes and wrote down the pattern.

It turned out the plug was loose.

That doesn’t mean there wasn’t a message before it came loose.

People think security is about buying expensive equipment and using complicated passwords. Sometimes it’s about noticing that your Christmas lights are behaving strangely while everyone else is distracted.

That’s how they get you.

I mentioned this in a server and somebody from ZeroDays reacted with a laughing face. Those skids wouldn’t recognise a live operation if it was blinking over their heads in three colours.

I checked his profile after that. Took me less than five minutes to build a complete picture.

I know what month he made the account, what picture he uses and at least two of his interests.

Basically everything.

I could have gone further but dinner was ready. We had roast chicken, and by the time I came back the conversation had moved on.

Probably for the best. Christmas is supposed to be peaceful.

I’ve still saved the light pattern, obviously.

Just in case.
