The Leak

June 16, 2026 2-minute read
PotKettleBlackHat
Computers • Life

Had a serious leak tonight.

Tea went all over my desk.

Luckily most of it missed the keyboard, although a notebook took a direct hit and now several pages of operational notes are permanently brown.

This is why I never keep everything digital. You can’t hack paper. You can spill tea on it, apparently, but that’s a separate threat model.

I spent twenty minutes moving cables and wiping everything down. The computer stayed on through the whole incident, which proves the setup is more resilient than most corporate networks.

ZeroDays would probably call that a successful stress test.

They’d make a logo for it and spend three weeks posting about how elite they are.

One of their skids tried to start something with me again recently. He said nobody took my claims seriously and asked for proof that I had ever hacked anything.

I told him the lack of proof was the proof.

If I left evidence everywhere, I wouldn’t be very good, would I?

He didn’t have an answer to that. He just posted another laughing face, which is what people do when they’ve lost but don’t want to admit it.

I could expose every member of ZeroDays before breakfast if I wanted. Names, locations, favourite takeaways, all of it. I don’t because then they would learn from their mistakes, and I prefer knowing more than they know I know.

That’s strategy.

After the desk was dry, I made another cup of tea and put it on the other side of the room. Had to stand up every time I wanted a drink, but security always comes with inconvenience.

The notebook might recover.

I’ll assess it in the morning.
