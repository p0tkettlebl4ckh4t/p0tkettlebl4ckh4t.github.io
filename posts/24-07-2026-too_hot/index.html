Too Hot

July 24, 2026 2-minute read
PotKettleBlackHat
Summer • Life

It has been too hot to do anything today.

I opened the window, closed the curtains and put a fan beside the desk, which just moved the warm air around faster.

At one point the computer sounded louder than usual. I put my hand near it and decided it was probably fine.

People worry too much about temperatures. Machines are designed to get hot. That’s why they have fans.

I was going to spend the afternoon sorting out some files, but instead I lay on the floor for a while because it was colder there. Ended up watching videos on my phone until the battery warning came on.

Very productive.

Later, somebody added me to a chat without asking. There were about twelve people in it and none of them would explain what it was for. One person just wrote, “We know who you are.”

I replied with his own profile picture.

He left immediately.

Classic counter-intelligence.

The others started removing me after that, so I told them it didn’t matter because I had already copied the room. I hadn’t, but they don’t know that, and knowing what the other side doesn’t know is half of hacking.

The chat disappeared a minute later.

Entire network dismantled from the floor of my bedroom during a heatwave.

Not my most difficult operation, but probably the least comfortable.

I went out for ice cream afterward. The shop only had two flavours left, so I got vanilla and ate it on the way home before it melted.

Supposed to be cooler tomorrow.

If it isn’t, all operations are postponed.
