End of Summer

August 26, 2026 2-minute read
PotKettleBlackHat
Life • Internet

Last day of August.

Summer always feels like it lasts forever until the final week, then suddenly it’s dark before you’ve finished dinner and every shop is selling school supplies.

I didn’t do half the things I planned.

Never went camping, never sorted the cupboard and still haven’t finished the game I started in May. I did buy a new water bottle, so the summer wasn’t completely wasted.

Spent this evening going through old messages and cleaning up a few accounts. There was a message request from someone claiming to be connected to ZeroDays.

He said they knew I had been talking about them.

Good.

I would be offended if they hadn’t noticed by now.

He asked me to stop calling them skids and said I didn’t understand what they actually did. I told him I understood perfectly. They organise themselves into a group because no single one of them has enough skill to open a settings menu alone.

He sent a paragraph back, but I didn’t read all of it.

You don’t negotiate with skids.

I told him I had been inside their network since last winter and left it there. This is technically true depending on what you define as a network and whether visiting a public page counts as being inside it.

Definitions matter in cyber operations.

He blocked me.

Another victory they won’t mention.

After that I went for a walk because the weather was still decent. It was quiet out and somebody nearby was cooking something that smelled incredible. Nearly knocked on the door to ask what it was, but even I have boundaries.

Back home now with the window open and a cup of tea beside me. Not too close to the notebook this time.

September tomorrow.

ZeroDays are still skids.

Some things don’t change.
