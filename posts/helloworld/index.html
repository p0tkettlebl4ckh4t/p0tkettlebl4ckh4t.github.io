Hello World

April 20, 2025 One-minute read
PotKettleBlackHat

Hello! Welcome to my blog.

I’ve been meaning to make one of these for ages but never really got around to it. Most of the stuff I do online ends up being random posts or messages that disappear after a few days, so I thought it would be nice to actually keep track of things somewhere.

I’ll probably write about whatever I’m doing, which could be anything from computers and random projects to films I’ve watched or places I’ve been.

I spent most of today cleaning my room because it had got to the point where I couldn’t find anything. Found three mugs, two chargers that I thought I’d lost, and about £7 in coins. So technically cleaning was profitable.

I also spent a couple of hours messing around online. Nothing particularly interesting happened, although somebody tried to tell me they could hide their identity because they were using a different username.

People really don’t understand how much information they leave lying around.

I could probably find most people’s details if I actually cared enough.

Anyway, that’s enough for a first post. I’m going to bed because it’s nearly 1am and I’ve got absolutely nothing planned tomorrow.

Probably the best possible start to a blog.
