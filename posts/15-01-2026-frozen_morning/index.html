Frozen Morning

January 15, 2026 2-minute read
PotKettleBlackHat
Life

It was freezing this morning.

I found this out after leaving the house in a hoodie because the sky looked bright through the window. That was a mistake.

The path was covered in ice and I nearly went straight over before I’d even closed the door. Managed to stay up, but only because I grabbed the wall in a way that probably looked very calm from a distance.

Got the bus into town and spent the whole journey trying to warm my hands up. The person behind me was watching videos without headphones, which should honestly be illegal before nine in the morning.

I considered shutting his phone down remotely.

Would have taken seconds.

I decided against it because the bus was crowded and I didn’t want to accidentally interfere with the ticket machine. That’s called operational awareness. A lot of so-called experts don’t have it.

I got coffee instead. The lid wasn’t on properly and some of it went down my sleeve, so that improved things.

The rest of the morning was normal. Picked up a few things, forgot one of the main things I went in for and came home with a notebook I didn’t need.

I’ve got about six empty notebooks now. I keep buying them because I imagine I’ll fill them with plans and important information.

Mostly I write one page and lose them.

This one is going to be different. I’ve already put a list of current operations on the first page. Most of them are classified, so the list is written in a way nobody else would understand.

It says “desk, cable, email, ZeroDays”.

I know what it means.

That’s enough.
